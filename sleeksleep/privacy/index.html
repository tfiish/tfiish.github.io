SleekSleep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

SleekSleep collects no data. It has no accounts, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party code, and it never sends anything off your iPhone on its own. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

What SleekSleep reads and writes

SleekSleep works only with Apple Health, and only with the sleep data you allow it to use.

  • It writes the sleep times you log, as Sleep entries in Apple Health.
  • It reads your Sleep data from Apple Health—including sleep saved by other apps and devices, if you allow it—to show your history and charts, and last night’s sleep on the widget.

All charts are worked out on your device while the app is open. SleekSleep does not keep its own copy of your sleep data.

What stays on your iPhone

SleekSleep keeps the following on your iPhone, in storage that only SleekSleep can read:

  • Your settings, such as your reminder time and sleep target.
  • An in-progress entry that hasn’t reached Apple Health yet (like an in-progress sleep, or an entry the SleekSleep widget couldn’t save). Each is removed as soon as it’s saved to Apple Health or discarded.

Exporting your sleep

If you choose Export CSV beside Download sleep data in Settings, SleekSleep makes a CSV file of the sleep it can read from Apple Health and hands it to the iOS share sheet. The file is kept in SleekSleep’s temporary storage only while the share sheet is open. It’s deleted when the sheet closes, or the next time you open SleekSleep if the app quit first. Where the file goes (such as AirDrop, an email, or your Files app) is your choice.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone. Nothing about them leaves your device.

Apple Health and iCloud

SleekSleep doesn’t use iCloud. If you’ve turned on Health in your iCloud settings, Apple syncs your Health data, including the entries SleekSleep saved, under the Apple Health privacy policy rather than this one.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete an entry SleekSleep logged in the app’s History, or delete it in the Health app.
  • Change or turn off SleekSleep’s access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → SleekSleep.
  • Delete the app to remove its settings and anything still waiting to be saved. Entries already saved stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
